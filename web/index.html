How it works

How to download a video with SaveVid.net

Four steps. It takes about as long as reading them.

1

Copy the video link

Open the video on the platform and copy its address. On a phone, tap the share icon and choose Copy link. SaveVid.net reads the link straight from your clipboard as soon as you tap the box, so you often never have to paste it yourself.

2

Paste it into the box

Tap the input field and paste. The title, thumbnail and every available quality appear within a couple of seconds. A recent lookup is served from cache, so a link you tried a moment ago comes back instantly.

3

Pick a quality or an audio format

Choose MP4 for video — 1080p, 720p, 480p and up to 4K when the original upload supports it. Choose MP3, WAV, M4A or FLAC if you only want the audio, extracted at the highest bitrate the source provides.

4

The download starts on its own

Click a format and your browser takes over immediately — the file goes straight to your downloads folder or gallery with no extra page in between. Nothing opens a new tab and nothing reloads.
